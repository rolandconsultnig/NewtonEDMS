# LogicalDoc — Function, Feature & Module Inventory

> Reference gathered from the official LogicalDoc product and architecture pages.

## 1. Gather / Capture & Upload
- **Mass upload** — bulk upload of many documents in one action
- **Import folders** — watched local/network folders that auto-import content
- **Email import** — archive emails and attachments from mailboxes
- **Microsoft Outlook add-in** — direct email/folder save to DMS
- **Scan to LogicalDoc** — TWAIN/scan ingestion
- **Multiple format support** — PDF, Office, CAD, DICOM, images, video, etc.
- **Data migration** — import from other DMS/file-systems

## 2. Index & Search
- **Automatic indexing** — metadata and full-text extraction
- **Multilingual full-text indexing**
- **OCR text recognition**
- **Zonal OCR**
- **Barcode / QR recognition**
- **Zonal barcode**
- **Kofax metadata extraction**
- **Advanced search** — keywords, tags, filters, Boolean operators
- **Faceted navigation**

## 3. Organize & Secure
- **Hierarchical folder tree** with categories and taxonomies
- **Metadata & templates**
- **Custom identifiers**
- **Retention policies** — long-term compliant archiving
- **Role-based access control (RBAC)**
- **Fine-grained permissions** (read, write, delete, manage, etc.)
- **Access control lists on folders and documents**
- **Automatic backup**
- **Import / export archives**

## 4. Versioning & Editing
- **Automatic versioning** / check-in & check-out
- **Document history & rollback**
- **Online editing**
- **Comments & annotations**
- **Real-time collaboration** (co-authoring)
